# Effects Map

This document maps configuration settings from `project.yaml` and `project.local.yaml` to their actual gameplay and automation effects in Pi Game Studio.

## modes.automation
Controls the autonomy level of agents. (collaborative, guided, autonomous)

## modes.rigor
Controls the strictness of testing and QA.

## platform.cert_tier
Target certification tier for the platform.

## Unimplemented Settings

> ### RESERVED
